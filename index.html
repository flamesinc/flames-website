Frequently Asked Questions

Everything commercial kitchen managers and owners need to know about transitioning to FLAMES.

FLAMES stoves feature a precision-tuned forced-draft combustion chamber. A small high-RPM air blower delivers oxygen directly into the gasification zone of the wood pellets, generating intense, concentrated heat up to 1000°C. This high thermal transfer rate heats large cooking vessels twice as fast as standard atmospheric commercial LPG burners.
The forced-draft blower requires minimal power (around 15 to 35 Watts, similar to a small mobile charger). It operates seamlessly on standard 220V AC wall plugs, small kitchen inverters, or a backup 12V DC battery, ensuring unhindered cooking even during power cuts.
FLAMES coordinates recurring doorstep deliveries in moisture-proof 25kg or 50kg bags. You can set an automated schedule (e.g. 500kg every fortnight) or order via a quick WhatsApp message to our delivery dispatch team. Deliveries are fulfilled rapidly across Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, and surrounding regions.
